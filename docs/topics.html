    About the topics

    The dictionary is not sorted by topic. This project sorted its entries by key words in their English meanings, so a list holds some words that do not belong and lacks some that do. Words to do with ceremony are not offered as a topic: they are waiting for review by the Pawnee community.

    Pâri Pakûru' — Skiri Pawnee Language Preservation Project. Data: Douglas R. Parks Dictionary & Blue Book Textbook. Built with respect for the Pawnee Nation.

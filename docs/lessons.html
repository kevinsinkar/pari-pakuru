The Blue Book — Pari Pakuru'

The best place to start learning Skiri Pawnee.

Pari Pakuru' ("Blue Book", 1979) was created by the Pawnee Cultural Retention Committee to keep the Skiri language alive — a practical course written by and for the Pawnee community, built around everyday questions and answers. Its 20 lessons are presented here in their original teaching order, and each in the book's own order: a few basic words, the dialogues that use them, more words, useful phrases and the notes on grammar. A word is linked to the Parks Dictionary wherever it could be tied to an entry, and each lesson ends with exercises made from its own lines.

The Blue Book's spelling differs from the dictionary's — that's normal, and nothing is wrong. Each lesson shows a word in the Blue Book's own spelling and, where the dictionary has it, in the dictionary's, with the simplified pronunciation (UPPERCASE = the accented syllable) and the phonetic form Parks recorded from fluent speakers. When spellings disagree about a sound, the phonetic form is the authority. The spelling to learn to write is the dictionary's learner spelling; every lesson page says which column that is.

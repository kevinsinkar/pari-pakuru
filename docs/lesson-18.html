KIRIKI RASUTSIKS•ARI'?

Lesson 18 — What Have You Been Doing?

Blue Book pp. 104–107

Why do the spellings differ?

The same Pawnee word appears in several writing systems, and they are all telling you the same thing:

  • Blue Book — the practical spelling printed in Pari Pakuru' (1979), exactly as the Pawnee Cultural Retention Committee wrote it. Some letters follow English habits (for example d for the soft Pawnee r).
  • Dictionary entry — the learner spelling from the Parks Dictionary, with a circumflex (â) marking long vowels.
  • Say it — the simplified pronunciation. UPPERCASE syllables carry high pitch, which changes meaning in Pawnee.
  • IPA — the exact scholarly transcription recorded by Douglas Parks from fluent speakers. When the other spellings disagree, the IPA is the authority.

Dialogues

1.. Tsikstit ka ras•pari '? Are you well?
Ahu ' , tsikstit tat•pari'. Yes, I'm fine.
Kirike rasutsiks•ari? What have you been doing?
ra +s +ut +uks-ari'
Rariksisu tatutsiks•a•wahri'. I've been working hard.

Dialogue lines are shown in the Blue Book's practical spelling, exactly as printed.

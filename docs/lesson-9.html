KIRK RA•'AT

Lesson 9 — Where Has It Gone?

Blue Book pp. 70–74

Why do the spellings differ?

The same Pawnee word appears in several writing systems, and they are all telling you the same thing:

  • Blue Book — the practical spelling printed in Pari Pakuru' (1979), exactly as the Pawnee Cultural Retention Committee wrote it. Some letters follow English habits (for example d for the soft Pawnee r).
  • Dictionary entry — the learner spelling from the Parks Dictionary, with a circumflex (â) marking long vowels.
  • Say it — the simplified pronunciation. UPPERCASE syllables carry high pitch, which changes meaning in Pawnee.
  • IPA — the exact scholarly transcription recorded by Douglas Parks from fluent speakers. When the other spellings disagree, the IPA is the authority.

Dialogues

Ka ra•awari rikutski?
Kaki', rikutski ti .'isat.
Kirti ra•'at rikutski?
Ti•kaisat.
Is the bird (still) flying around?
No, the bird has disappeared.
Where has the bird gone?
It flew into the woods.

Objects

ra .hak•tsa a log, the log ti hak•tsa that's a log karit•kutsu' boulder (big rock) Rahurahki we tut•awi 'at The deer jumped over karit•kutsu'. the big rock. NOUNS IN THE SENTENCE: WORD ORDER Remember, there are two main kinds of nouns you may find in a sentence, subjects (refer to who is doing it) and objects (refer to who or what is experiencing it, but not doing anything). Kiwaku we tu'h•kuksas . The fox ran away.

Object Verb Phrase

If there is only one noun in the sentence, it can come either before or after the verb, according to what is the most important new information in the sentence. Rikutski ti pahaat. The bird is red. Ti pahaat rikutski. The bird is red. Normally, what is important or in focus is first in the sentence; what is understood or presumed is last. When two nouns appear in the same sentence, the subject comes directly before the verb phrase, the object comes last, and time words may follow the object. Pita ti•kuwutit rahurahki pitsikat. subject verb phr. object time word The man killed a deer , in the winter.

Week 15: Water & Rivers

15 words. Click a card to flip it. “Got it” and “Missed” also tell the Practice page when to bring a word back.

Which spelling do I learn to write? The large word on the card: the learner spelling, with â î û for long vowels, ' for the catch in the throat, c for “ts” and č for “ch”, which is the spelling of the Pawnee Nation's own lesson handouts. A smaller word under it is the same word in the dictionary's letters, and the line in capitals and hyphens is a rough guide for English speakers, not a spelling. A card whose back names the textbook and no dictionary page is a word found only in the 1979 textbook: it is written as the textbook spells it, in the dictionary's letters (ʔ for '). Where a teacher or a speaker writes a word another way, follow them.

Skiri or South Band? The dictionary's words are Skiri. A card whose back names the textbook is spelled the textbook's way, and the textbook's spelling follows South Band speakers: where it writes e, a Skiri speaker says i.

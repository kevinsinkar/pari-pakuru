Weekly Flashcards

A 20-week study plan with 387 curated words across 21 categories. Each week features 15-20 beginner-friendly words, prioritizing terms from the Pâri Pakûru' textbook.

How to read a flashcard

UPPERCASE syllables have HIGH PITCH. Pitch changes meaning in Pawnee, so pay attention to which syllable is capitalized.

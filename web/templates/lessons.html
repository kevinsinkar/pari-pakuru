The Blue Book — Pari Pakuru'

The best place to start learning Skiri Pawnee.

Pari Pakuru' ("Blue Book", 1979) was created by the Pawnee Cultural Retention Committee to keep the Skiri language alive — a practical course written by and for the Pawnee community, built around everyday questions and answers. Its 20 lessons are presented here in their original teaching order: each one gives you the vocabulary, dialogues, grammar notes, and exercises exactly as the book teaches them, with every word linked to the Parks Dictionary.

The Blue Book's spelling differs from the dictionary's — that's normal, and nothing is wrong. Each lesson shows the word in the Blue Book's own spelling and the dictionary spelling, side by side with the simplified pronunciation (UPPERCASE = high pitch) and the IPA transcription recorded from fluent speakers. When spellings disagree, trust your ear and the IPA.

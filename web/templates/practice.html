Practice

Six short games over the same words as the flashcards, the seven verbs the dictionary writes out in full, and the pieces words are built from. Ten questions a round. A little every day does more than a lot once a week, so the page keeps track of which words are due today.

About these games

Every Skiri form in a question or an answer is printed in the dictionary or the textbook; a game never makes a form. The words are the flashcard words, chosen by this project and not yet reviewed by a speaker.

How the page remembers. Each flashcard word you answer goes into one of five boxes. A word you get right comes back later each time: the next day, then after three days, a week, two weeks and a month. A word you miss goes back to the first box and is asked again the same day. “Today's words” asks the words that are due, and fills the round with new ones in the order of the flashcard weeks. The flashcards' “Got it” and “Missed” move a word the same way. All of it is kept in this browser only: on another device, or after the browser's data is cleared, the boxes start empty.

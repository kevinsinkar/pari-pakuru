Practice

Six short games over the same words as the flashcards, the seven verbs the dictionary writes out in full, and the pieces words are built from. Ten questions a round. A little every day does more than a lot once a week.

About these games

Every Skiri form in a question or an answer is printed in the dictionary or the textbook; a game never makes a form. The words are the flashcard words, chosen by this project and not yet reviewed by a speaker. The words you miss are remembered in this browser only, so that you can practise them again.

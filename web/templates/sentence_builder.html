Sentence Builder

Start from a sentence the Blue Book prints. Swap a word for another word the book prints. The page tells you whether the result is a sentence of the book.

What you can trust here
  • Every Blue Book word is copied from the book as read from the scan, with its accent. Nothing is typed in or worked out.
  • "Printed in the Blue Book, lesson N" means the whole sentence stands in the book, accents included. "Put together here; not printed" means the words are the book's but the sentence is not: nobody has checked it, and a swapped word keeps the accent it had where it came from.
  • Dictionary verbs are in the second part, in Parks's spelling, and never mixed into a Blue Book sentence. A form marked "derived; not checked against a printed form" was made from a printed form by rule. It has no accent mark.

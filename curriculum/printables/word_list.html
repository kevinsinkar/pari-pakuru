Pâri Pakûru' — word list

The flashcard words of the study site, week by week. Made 14 November 2026 from two books: Parks and Pratt, A Dictionary of Skiri Pawnee (2008), and Pari Pakuru' (1979). “p.” is a page of the dictionary; “textbook” marks a word taken from Pari Pakuru'.

The learner spelling writes a long vowel with a circumflex and the glottal stop with an apostrophe. The guide to saying a word is rough, made from Parks's phonetic form by fixed rules; capitals mark the accented syllable. No speaker has reviewed this list yet.

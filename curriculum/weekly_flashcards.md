**Total: 372 words, 995 cards over 24 weeks (2026-11-08 to 2027-04-18).**

Generated by `scripts/build_weekly_flashcards.py` from the frozen curriculum `curriculum/flashcard_sets.json` (version 1, frozen 2026-10-12). Week 1 is Sunday 2026-11-08; each later week is the next Sunday. Write the week's cards on Sunday, then review them during the week (see the schedule below).

How a word is built, and which compounds are offered on C cards, comes from the word-construction analysis in `curriculum/roots_v2/`. A meaning is printed beside a piece only where Parks glosses that piece by itself in the word, or where the analysis and Parks's translation of the word agree on it. Otherwise the piece is shown bare: that means "no meaning confirmed", not "no meaning".

## How the cards are designed

- **Labels in capitals** are Parks's grammatical labels for pieces that have a job, not a dictionary meaning: ABS = absolutive mode prefix; ACT = activizer suffix; DIM = diminutive suffix; DIST = distributive suffix; IMPF = imperfective aspect suffix; LOC = locative/directional prefix or prefix string; NOM = nominal/absolutive suffix; PERF = perfective aspect suffix; PL/ITER = plural prefix; PORT = portative prefix; PREV = preverb; TRANS = transitivizer affix.
- **One fact per card.** A word gets two core cards: **A** (see Pawnee → recall meaning) and **B** (see English → produce Pawnee). Testing both directions is what makes a word usable, not just recognisable.
- **Pronunciation sits with the Pawnee word**, never alone on the front: on card A it is part of the cue (read it aloud before flipping), on card B it is part of the answer (say it, then check).
- **Backs are short.** Meaning, word class, a source reference, and, when Parks gives one, how the word is built from its pieces. The source lets you check the dictionary later instead of trusting the card.
- **Forms go on their own cards (C).** For verbs, each attested dictionary form is a separate card cued by its meaning ('he/she …', 'I …', 'is …-ing', 'is going to …'). For nouns, up to two attested compounds built on the same root are extra cards. Treat C cards as optional: add them in a later week if the A/B cards are already solid.
- **Spelling.** Cards use the learner spelling (*â î û* long vowels, *'* glottal stop, *č* = ch, *c* = ts). The back of card A gives Parks's dictionary spelling and page for lookup. Pronunciations without ≈ come from Parks's phonetic transcription; ≈ marks a spelling-based respelling of an inflected form (no stress shown). CAPITALS mark Parks's accented syllable.
- **Review schedule.** Sunday: write and read through once. Monday: all cards. Tuesday: misses only. Thursday: all cards. Saturday: all cards plus a shuffled handful from every earlier week. A card answered correctly three reviews in a row moves to a monthly pile.

# Learning path: the building blocks of Skiri Pawnee, most useful first

Generated by `scripts/morphology_v2/run.py --guide`. Every Pawnee form is looked up from a source record (see `construction_guide.md` for how to read sources, tiers and spellings).

## How the order was worked out

1. **What counts.** Only morphemes of tier A or B, and only words whose analysis is tier A or B and whose every piece has a meaning that was checked in that word. That leaves 579 teachable building blocks and 1791 words that can be taken apart completely.
2. **Prerequisites.** A word is shown only after every one of its pieces has been taught, and a recipe only after the pieces it needs.
3. **Which block next.** Each block is credited, for every word it helps build, with that word's weight divided by the number of its pieces still untaught. A word the Blue Book teaches weighs 2.0, any other word 1.0. The credit is multiplied by 1 + 0.5 × the share of the block's words that join with no sound change. The block with the highest credit is taught next.
4. **Sensitivity.** Each weight was shifted by ±25%, alone and together. The first 50 blocks keep a rank correlation of 0.992 or better with this order, and at least 98% of them stay in the first 50. The order does not hinge on the weights.
5. **Community review.** Words whose meaning is ceremonial or sacred are not used as teaching words in this path. They are listed in `review_queue.md` for the Pawnee community to decide on.

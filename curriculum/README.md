# curriculum/

Learner material built from the database. Everything here is generated by a
script except the two files that record a decision: the frozen flashcard list
and the list of weeks held for review.

| Item | What it is | Made by |
|---|---|---|
| `flashcard_sets.json` | **The flashcard curriculum**: 24 weekly sets, 387 words, frozen on 2026-10-12. The web app and the static site both read this file | `scripts/freeze_flashcard_sets.py --apply` |
| `held_for_review.json` | Weeks of that curriculum that are not shown anywhere until they have been reviewed. Now: week 20, "Ceremony & Sacred" | edited by hand |
| `weekly_flashcards.md` | The curriculum as paper cards (front and back), one section per week, Week 1 = Sunday 2026-11-08. 372 words, 995 cards; the held week has a placeholder | `scripts/build_weekly_flashcards.py --start 2026-11-08` |
| `flashcard_pdfs/` | Those cards as printable sheets, four to a page, double-sided: one PDF per week and `all_weeks.pdf` | `scripts/build_flashcard_pdfs.py` |
| [`roots_v2/`](roots_v2/) | **The current word-building guide** and all the evidence behind it | `scripts/morphology_v2/run.py --all` |
| [`roots/`](roots/) | Version 1 of the word-building guide (2026-11-07). **Superseded**; kept for the reasons below | `scripts/root_extraction.py`, `scripts/build_root_curriculum.py` |

## The flashcard list is frozen

Learners' progress on the static site is stored in their browser under the
week number. If the list were rebuilt from the database on every build, any
change to the data would shuffle the weeks and attach progress to the wrong
cards. So the list is a file, and it changes only when someone runs the freeze
script deliberately and commits the result.

Repairs to a word's data (its IPA, respelling or page number) are copied into
the frozen file by the script that makes the repair. Membership and week
numbers are never touched by a repair.

## Holding a week back

Week 20 holds ceremonial vocabulary: sacred bundles, dances, doctoring. Whether
that belongs in a flashcard deck is for the Pawnee Nation to say, and nobody
has asked yet. Until then the week is listed in `held_for_review.json`, and:

- the web app does not list it, and answers "not found" for its page;
- the static site build writes no page for it;
- the paper cards have a placeholder in its place and no PDF.

The words stay in `flashcard_sets.json` and every other week keeps its number
and its date. To release the week, delete its entry from `held_for_review.json`
and rebuild the site and the cards.

## What the paper cards say about how a word is built

Since 2026-11-13 the line "built from …" on an A card, and the optional C cards
for nouns, come from the version 2 analysis (`roots_v2/evidence.jsonl`).

- The pieces are Parks's printed etymology. They are shown only when they
  reproduce the headword.
- A piece has a meaning beside it only when Parks glosses that piece by itself
  in that word, or when two readings agree: the meaning the analysis checked,
  and the gloss stored with the piece or Parks's translation of the whole word.
- Where the readings disagree, or Parks gives the piece no meaning, the piece
  is shown bare. Bare means "no meaning confirmed". Those are the cases for a
  speaker or linguist to settle.
- A C card is offered only when the root has the same checked meaning in both
  words, and never for a word flagged for community review.

Compared with the cards built from version 1 on 2026-11-08: the two core cards
of each word (A front, B) are unchanged, except that 45 words now show their
accented syllable in capitals because the accent had been lost from the data.
On the backs of A cards, 56 "built from" lines changed in wording, 45 words
gained a line, and labels are shorter ('NOM' for 'NOM -uʔ'). About half of the
optional C cards are different words.

## Two word-building guides

| | `roots/` (version 1) | `roots_v2/` (version 2) |
|---|---|---|
| Date | 2026-11-07 | 2026-11-12, re-run 2026-11-13 |
| Reads Parks's etymologies from | the stored parse, whose per-piece meanings are often on the wrong piece | the printed etymology, re-parsed |
| Checks an analysis against | the headword only | the headword, the verb's forms, and Blue Book seams |
| Counts a root in | every headword whose stored parse contains the shape | words whose analysis was confirmed and where the root's meaning was checked |
| Lesson order | set by hand | computed, and tested for stability |
| Tested on | nothing held back | 120 hand-checked entries, half held back |

**Use `roots_v2/`.** Version 1 is still here because version 2 reads its files
to write the list of what changed (`roots_v2/changelog_vs_v1.md`) and to
stratify its test sample, and because two version 1 scripts are still imported
by live code for their spelling helpers. Nothing a learner sees is built from
version 1 any more.

## Rules for anything written here

- Every Pawnee form is looked up from the database, never typed.
- IPA appears only where Parks prints it for that exact word.
- Blue Book forms are quoted in the Blue Book's spelling.
- Ceremonial vocabulary stays out until the community has reviewed it.
